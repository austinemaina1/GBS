Quarry to Site, Under One Roof

GBS is a quarry materials and haulage company — we supply the aggregate and we move it, so nothing gets lost between a quote and a delivery.

Who We Are

GBS — Generation Builders & Suppliers — supplies construction aggregates: ballast, hardcore and sand, sourced and graded at the quarry, then hauled directly to your site on our own fleet of dump trucks.

Most material supply chains hand your order off between a quarry, a broker and a separate haulage contractor — any one of which can stall a delivery. We keep sourcing and haulage under one roof, so a delivery window we give you is a window we control.

  • One Point of Contact

    From your order to the truck arriving on site, you're dealing with GBS, not a chain of subcontractors.

  • Haulage We Own

    Our TATA dump trucks are ours to schedule, so a confirmed delivery time stays confirmed.
