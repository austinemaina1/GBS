Our Materials

Six core materials, graded and checked at the quarry before they're loaded for your site.

Aggregate

Ballast

Crushed stone screened at the quarry for concrete work and structural fill. Ordered by the truckload and checked for consistent grading before dispatch, so what arrives matches what you specced.

Aggregate

Rocksand

Crushed rock sand for concrete mixing, masonry and plastering work. Screened at the quarry for a consistent, workable grade.

Aggregate

Dust

Fine quarry dust for blinding, backfilling and levelling work. Same quarry-direct sourcing as our other aggregates, so quality stays consistent order to order.

Aggregate

Hardcore

Solid broken stone for foundations, slabs and access roads. Delivered in bulk and timed to keep your compaction and sub-base work moving without gaps.

Building Stone

Foundation Stones

Sized stone for foundation walling and footing work, sourced and staged at the quarry ahead of your delivery.

Building Stone

Machine Cut Stone

Uniform machine-cut blocks, ready for walling and construction — delivered by the truckload alongside your other materials.
